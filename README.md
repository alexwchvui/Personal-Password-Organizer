    Personal-Password-Organizer/Work/

(2) Double Click the [personal-password-organizer.html] to open Browser.

(3) Create [Master Password] and click [Create Vault] to start Login.

(4) After Login, Fill the [Tile Name] , [Link] , [Email] , [Password] , [Note] , [Upload Image],
    Then click the [Save Tile] to save the information.

(5) To Backup, please click the [Choose Folder] button to select the backup folder.

(6) Click the [Backup] butoon to backup all information.

(7) To Restore, click the [Restore] button and select the backup file.

(8) To logout, click the [Logout] button.

(9) To clear all the information , click the [Erase Local Vault] at the Main Page.

(10) At the Main Page, click to [Import Backup] , select the backup file to recover your information.
